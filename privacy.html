This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit mygreenlawn, how it is used, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

The short version: the checklist saves your ticks in your own browser's local storage. We do not have accounts, do not run advertising trackers, and do not sell personal information.

1. Overview

mygreenlawn is a static informational website about lawn care. You can read the entire guide, use the watering calculator, and check off the maintenance list without registering or providing your name, email address, or any other identifying detail.

This policy applies to the mygreenlawn website and its pages. It does not apply to third-party sites you may reach through links.

2. What we collect

Information you choose to give us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use that only to read and respond to your note. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the server or hosting provider that delivers this site may process basic technical information in standard logs, such as:

  • IP address and approximate region;
  • browser type, device type, and operating system;
  • pages requested and the date and time of the request;
  • referring page and error codes.

These logs are used to keep the site working, diagnose problems, and understand aggregate traffic. We do not use them to identify individual visitors.

Information we do not collect

  • No account registration or passwords.
  • No payment or financial information.
  • No precise geolocation.
  • No advertising profiles or cross-site behavioral tracking.

3. How we use information

Where information is available to us, we use it to:

  • deliver and maintain the website;
  • respond to messages you send us;
  • monitor reliability, fix errors, and prevent abuse;
  • understand aggregate usage so we can improve the guide;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Cookies and local storage

mygreenlawn does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The site does use your browser's local storage for one purpose: remembering which checklist items you have ticked.

  • The checklist data is stored under the key mygreenlawn.checklist.v1 in your browser.
  • It stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • It remains until you clear your browser data or press Reset list on the guide.
  • Clearing site data or using a private window will remove or isolate it.

If you prefer not to have this stored, simply don't use the checklist, or clear it after your visit. Your browser settings also let you block or delete local storage.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and operations. Those logs are controlled by the provider and are retained for a limited period under its own policies.

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be disclosed only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers. A hosting or email provider may process data on our behalf to run the site or deliver messages.
  • Legal requirements. If required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the site.
  • Business changes. If the site is transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may be transferred along with it, subject to this policy.

Aggregate, non-identifying statistics may be shared for general reporting.

6. Data retention

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, then deleted. Server logs are retained by the hosting provider for a limited operational period. Checklist data stays in your browser until you remove it; we never hold a copy.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we collect very little, the most common request is simply deletion of an email you sent us.

To make a request, contact hello@mygreenlawn.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined. If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases for processing are legitimate interests (operating and securing the site) and consent or contract where applicable.

8. Children's privacy

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the site and any information we hold, including using reputable hosting and limiting collection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive information by email.

10. International visitors

The site may be hosted in a country other than yours. If you access it from outside that country, your connection may involve the transfer of technical data across borders. We aim to handle any such data consistently with this policy and applicable law.

11. Third-party links

The guide may link to external resources such as extension services, equipment retailers, or reference material. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Review their policies before providing personal information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The effective date at the top of the page will change when we do. If a change is material, we will make it clear on this page. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy? Email hello@mygreenlawn.com.
